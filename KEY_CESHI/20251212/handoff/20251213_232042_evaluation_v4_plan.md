## Recommended Additional Metrics

### Priority 1: EUR vs Baseline Comparison (⭐⭐⭐)

**Goal**: Prove EUR outperforms random/Sobol sampling

**Implementation**: Monte Carlo comparison with 10+ repeats

**Metrics**:
- EUR F1 vs Baseline mean F1
- Improvement percentage
- Statistical significance (t-test)

**Why critical**: This is THE key validation answering "how much better is EUR?"

### Priority 2: Statistical Power Analysis (⭐⭐)

**Goal**: Assess if sample size is sufficient to detect effects

**Implementation**: Use `statsmodels.stats.power.tt_solve_power`

**Metrics**:
- Mean power across effects
- Count of underpowered effects (power < 0.8)

**Why important**: Standard statistical metric, reviewers will ask

## FAQ

**Q: Which is more important, v3 or v4?**  
A: Complementary. v4 evaluates "can we find the right interactions", v3 evaluates "are coefficients accurate". Use both.

**Q: Why EUR vs Baseline comparison?**  
A: Proves EUR value. If EUR F1=0.6, random F1=0.5, then EUR improves 20%. This is the key selling point.

**Q: What is statistical power for?**  
A: Answers "is sample size enough". If power < 0.8, even with EUR, sample size is insufficient to reliably detect effects.

**Q: Is model comparison reliable with tiny samples (n=3-5)?**  
A: BIC penalizes complex models. With very small samples, all models may have similar BIC, and v4 will warn "no significant difference". This itself is useful information.
